Lightning-fast remote desktop in your browser: AV1 decoded by the browser, multi-user sessions with xrdp, no client to install.

A gift from a hobby to the world, for VDI, HPC, AI, VPS, research and the community: free and open source on GitHub.

An unofficial community client, not written by security professionals. Keep the RDP server on 127.0.0.1, reach it through SSH and stop the gateway after the session.
