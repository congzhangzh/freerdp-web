Release notes

What changed for you, newest first. The version you are running is shown in a session's Info panel, on the Client line. A new version arrives the next time you load the page; a session that is already open keeps going.

0.4.5 · 18 October 2026

  • The same header on every page: FreeRDP Web as the title, then ▶ Intro, the help pages, Release notes and the theme, in one row. ▶ Intro on a help page takes you to the demo videos.

0.4.4 · 18 October 2026

  • This page: what changed for you in each version, linked at the top of every page.
  • The header's items (▶ Intro, the help pages, Release notes, the theme) share one style.

0.4.3 · 18 October 2026

  • The SSH line in the safety notice and on Safety and tunnels now starts with ssh -tt. The gateway on your server then stops by itself when you close the tunnel, even if you started ssh from a script or a shortcut.

0.4.2 · 18 October 2026

  • The SSH line now sends your local gateway port to the server's port 3390, where websockify runs next to RDP. If you use another port on your computer, copy the line again.
  • If you never changed a host's gateway, it moves from 127.0.0.1:6080 to the new default 127.0.0.1:3390 by itself. Gateways you typed yourself stay as they are.
  • ▶ Intro in the header shows the demo videos again.

0.4.1 · 18 October 2026

  • The default gateway is 127.0.0.1:3390, next to RDP's 3389, in the page, the help and rdp-gateway.sh.

0.4.0 · 18 October 2026

  • Two one-minute demos on your first visit: Xfce through xrdp, and GNOME on Wayland through gnome-remote-desktop, with web video, WebGL and sound on the remote desktop. Skip them if you like; they download only when they play.
  • Theme: System, Light or Dark, at the top of every page.
  • A new address: congzhangzh.github.io/freerdp-web. The old one forwards here.

0.3.0 · 18 October 2026

  • Hosts: keep several servers in the bar under the safety notice. Each remembers its gateway, user, session settings and advanced options; the form edits the selected one as you type. Your earlier settings became the first host.
  • The passkey vault, per host (Advanced): keep a host's password encrypted in this browser and unlock it with a passkey when you connect. Otherwise your browser's password manager keeps it.
  • Export and import your hosts as a file to move them to another browser, with the vault's passwords only if you tick it, and encrypted with a passphrase if you tick that.

0.2.0 · 18 October 2026

  • A simpler start: gateway, user, password, Connect. The browser's password manager offers to save the password.
  • The route at the top: how the page reaches your desktop, with the one SSH line that sets it up.
  • Session settings: sound, clipboard, full screen, the video codec, and a priority: save bandwidth, or reduce the server's load.
  • Advanced: Direct TCP in the installed app, the passkey vault, extra FreeRDP options.
  • Keyboard: Windows Remote Desktop (mstsc) shortcuts if your hands know those: Keyboard.
  • The remote desktop now fills the window; before, it was drawn at 800 × 600.

0.1 · October 2026

  • The first version: FreeRDP's client in your browser through a WebSocket gateway, or with Direct TCP in the installed app; clipboard text and images, full screen with the keyboard captured, sound, the session toolbar and Info panel, and a saved log when a session ends unexpectedly.

For developers: the detailed changes are in CHANGELOG.md.
